## 📖 What is Zara AI Laptop Agent?

Zara is a personal AI assistant that runs on your Windows laptop and connects to your phone through Discord. You can talk to Zara in voice notes (English or Urdu), type commands, and ask it to do things like take screenshots, manage files, run programs, and control your desktop. Think of Zara as your smart helper that lives inside your computer.

Whether you are at your desk or away, Zara lets you stay in control. Just message it on Discord and it follows your instructions.

## ✨ Features

- **Screenshot on demand** - Ask Zara to capture your screen and share the image.
- **File management** - Find, open, move, delete, and rename files.
- **Application control** - Launch programs, switch windows, or close apps.
- **Command execution** - Run system commands through safe automation.
- **Voice notes support** - Speak in English or Urdu. Zara understands your voice.
- **Text-to-speech** - Zara can read responses aloud.
- **Always ready** - Zara listens for your instructions 24/7 while the app runs.

## ❓ Troubleshooting

**Zara doesn't respond**  
Check your internet and Discord connection. Restart Zara.

**Voice not recognized**  
Speak clearly and check your microphone.

**Install fails**  
Make sure your Windows is updated. Run the installer as Admin.
